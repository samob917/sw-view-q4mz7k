CCPSA Schedule

Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-08 · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: this app holds what it needs to run your practice's schedule. We don't sell it and don't use it for advertising. If you leave analytics on, we see how the app is used, with names, text and anything you type hidden, so we can find and fix problems. You can turn analytics off at any time from the Account button in the app.

Who we are

This app is the scheduling app for CCPSA. Scheduling Wizard, Inc. builds and runs it for CCPSA under our agreement with the practice. This policy covers this app only. Our website, schedulingwiz.com, has its own separate policy.

We act for your practice. Your practice decides who has an account and how the schedule is run. We handle your information on its behalf.

What we hold

  • Your account: your name, role and work email. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash. Your two-factor key is stored encrypted. If you use Face ID or a fingerprint, we keep only the public key your device creates; your biometrics never leave your device.
  • The schedule: your shifts, time off and requests, swaps, cover, and the notes and messages you write in the app.
  • Your devices: when you last signed in, which devices are signed in, and, if you turn them on, the details needed to send notifications to your device and your private calendar link.
  • Usage analytics, only if you agree and leave it on: see below.

Usage analytics and session recording

With your agreement, we use PostHog (a product analytics service, data held in the United States) to see which screens are used, what errors happen, and short recordings of how the app behaved. This is how we find bugs that are hard to reproduce, especially on phones.

  • You are known by a random code, not your name or email.
  • All text on screen and everything you type is hidden before anything is sent. Recordings show the layout of the app, not its content.
  • Recording stops completely while you are signing in, setting up two-factor, writing a note or message, or attaching a file.
  • We do not collect your IP address, network traffic or browser console.
  • Recordings are deleted after 30 days.

Turn it off at any time: tap Account in the app, then set Usage analytics to Off. When it is off, nothing is loaded or sent, and any analytics data stored in your browser is cleared.

How we use it

  • To show you and your colleagues the schedule, and to handle requests, swaps and cover.
  • To send the emails and notifications the app sends (for example, a swap waiting for you).
  • To keep accounts secure, and to help when someone can't sign in.
  • To find and fix problems and improve the app.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • People at your practice: colleagues see the schedule. Administrators and scheduling staff also see requests, swaps and account status.
  • Scheduling Wizard: our team, to run and support the app.
  • Services we use to run the app: Vercel (hosting and storage), PostHog (analytics, only if on), Google (sending email), and your phone or browser maker's notification service (only if you turn notifications on). They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • If the law requires it: we will disclose information when we are legally required to.

Patient information

This app is for staff scheduling. Please don't enter patient names or any other patient information in notes, messages or requests.

How long we keep it

Account and schedule records are kept while your practice uses the app, and then returned or deleted as our agreement with the practice sets out. Session recordings are deleted after 30 days.

Security

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. Accounts need a password and two-factor sign-in. Sessions expire, and you can sign out all your devices from the Account button in the app.

Your choices

  • Turn analytics on or off at any time from the Account button in the app.
  • To see, correct or delete information about you, ask your practice's scheduling lead or email us. Some schedule records belong to the practice, so we will work with them on requests about those.

Changes

If this policy changes, the version date above changes and the app asks you to agree again the next time you open it.

Contact

Questions or requests: team@schedulingwiz.com
